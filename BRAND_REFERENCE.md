# ACCS brand reference

Source: [CAPSTONE (ACCS): DESIGNS/BRANDING](https://www.figma.com/design/HhZBfgDKt4aMudftxOP4Jg/CAPSTONE--ACCS---DESIGNS-BRANDING), inspected 2026-10-19. This records visible Figma content; it does not approve a logo or finalize interface tokens.

## Identity

- Name: **ACCS** — “Access for College of Computer Studies”; pronounced “Access.”
- Brand guide tagline: **Your Access to the College of Computer Studies**.
- Personality: professional, trustworthy, modern, accessible, structured.
- Voice: community driven and encouraging in the forum; empathetic and clear in student support; serious, secure, and confidential in grievance flows.

## Type and imagery

- Display/headings: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, or Outfit suggested in the Moodboard.
- Body: Inter or Roboto suggested in the Moodboard.
- Code snippets: JetBrains Mono suggested in the Moodboard.
- Five frames named “Logo Proposal 1” through “Logo Proposal 5” appear on Assets/Branding. The brand guide artwork shows a blue linked-C mark and ACCS wordmark. No proposal is labeled approved.
- Moodboard includes visual inspiration and logo sketches. Assets/Branding includes logo proposals, a brand guide cover, logo mockups, and the palette frame.
- Wireframes and High Fidelity pages had no visible layers at inspection.

Before using a logo or font as a final asset, obtain the team’s selection and export the chosen original from Figma. Avoid substituting a screenshot of the proposals for an approved logo.
