# ACCS landing v2 rebuild

Status: in progress on branch `redesign/v2`. Master: this delta.

Rebuild the landing site from a scaffold, without `@meetingat10/design-system`, Tailwind or Motion. Keep the content, routes, anchors, build-time URLs and static export. Only the visual language and the components change.

## Direction

Wayfinding for a college. ACCS is pronounced "access", so the site reads like the signs and route map of a public transit system: where you can go, what your role lets you enter, and who handles a concern.

- Ground: Sign Blue `#032E87` for the hero and sign bands, Navy `#172033` for ink, cool paper `#F3F6FB`. In dark mode only the paper sections change; sign bands keep their blue.
- Accent: Signal Yellow `#FFC629` for the primary action and "you are here". Seven line colors exist only to identify the seven workspaces.
- Type: Archivo with its width axis (expanded for signs, condensed for map labels), Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for references and times.
- Shape: square corners and 45 degree cuts. Only stations are round.
- Signature: the route map. One college account fans out into seven lines, each stop is one module, and choosing a role lights the stops that role can use.

## Sections

Hero sign with a role board, route map with strip maps and working previews (`#sites`), pictogram wall (`#features`), concern route with the adviser bypass (`#guidance`), request staircase (`#workflow`), programs (`#programs`), thread list (`#forum`), departures board (`#events`), final sign, footer. `/modules/` keeps every module, its purpose, what it does, the role index and the workspace filter.

## Acceptance

- `npm run verify`, lint and typecheck pass. All 58 module names, the 11 role labels and the seven workspaces render in static HTML on both routes.
- No design-system dependency or `ds-` class. No picture assets. No document codes, module IDs or pending-approval notes in visible copy.
- Public links stay on the allowed portal paths. Restricted workspaces link only to sign-in.
- Checked in the browser at 1440, 1024 and 390 wide, light and dark, with reduced motion, keyboard focus and no console errors.
